Privacy

      
When you press Scan, the extension takes one screenshot of the stream video. With on-device AI it never leaves your computer. Otherwise it is sent to the service you chose (our card-ID service, Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude) only to identify the card, and nothing is stored. Card names are looked up on pokemontcg.io and TCGCSV, and graded prices on PokemonPriceTracker. Keys you enter are stored only in this browser.
